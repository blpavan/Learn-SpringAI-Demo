KnowledgeDesk Support FAQ

Password resets: Users can reset their password from the account security page.
If multi-factor authentication is enabled, they must complete MFA verification
before choosing a new password.

VPN access: Employees should use the company VPN when accessing internal systems
from outside the office. If the VPN client fails to connect, restart the client,
confirm network access, and contact support if the issue continues.

Hardware requests: New laptop, monitor, keyboard, and mouse requests require
manager approval before the IT support team can fulfill the ticket.
